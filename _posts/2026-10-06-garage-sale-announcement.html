The Sonic Squirrels FRC Team 2930 are hosting a garage sale on November 13th from 6:00–8:00 PM in the Commons at Glacier Peak High School.

After losing one of our storage spaces, we have been sorting through our collection of robotics equipment, tools, and parts. We are opening up some of that equipment to the community rather than letting useful items sit in storage.

Everything is being sold as-is and items will be available on a first-come, first-served basis. Payment methods and item pricing will be confirmed before the sale.

Location: Glacier Peak High School Commons
7401 144th Place SE, Snohomish, WA 98296
Date: November 13th
Time: 6:00–8:00 PM

Purchases help support the Sonic Squirrels and our continued participation in FIRST Robotics Competition. Thanks for supporting local student robotics!
